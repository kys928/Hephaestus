## Two separate experiment scopes

1. **Existing internal generated-task science:** the completed original contiguous CPU 45/45, 96 actual optimizer steps and 0/24 to 2/24 exact correctness were valid only under the original generated Tier A research policy. They are NOT an independent historical-reference certification and the evaluation split is already exposed through prior one-shot confirmation. **A Tier A CUDA service adapter for that same generated-data research route is not yet implemented in this PR.**
2. **Historical-source independent review path:** the new original-CUDA composer wires the existing full-corpus `IndependentDatasetReviewPolicy` / `StagedApprovedHuggingFaceDataFactory`. It requires externally pinned original rights, screened source/index and tokenizer/row mapper, baseline, recipe and human-scoped acquisition approval. **No actual historical training source has yet passed those gates.** Do not introduce a fake review to obtain an execution trace.

Scientific certification custody stays a **separate subsequent gate**: original private training/development/diagnostic/holdout text, blinded independent semantic leakage audit, Granite comparisons, and externally verifiable human signature. Source admission is not a certificate, and GPU execution is not a certificate.
