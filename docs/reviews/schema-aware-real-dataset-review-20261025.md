## What the new work proves (and does NOT prove)

1. Real public schema inspection and fixed source-specific mappings work. JSON completion output and generated input parameter schema are **not interchangeable**. SAF student responses and feedback must **not** be mistaken for gold answer targets.
2. The frozen tokenizer's unknown rate is measured **before and after** deterministic filter/normalization, without modifying the tokenizer or the 1% threshold. English filtering changed the observable subset; cross-population performance and source-language reliability remain unknown.
3. WikiText's 40 windows prove only a **within-row contiguous tokenization recipe** on 100 preview rows. They do not prove benchmark-novelty, document boundaries, decontamination, EOS labels, semantic evaluation, or the right to train on the source.
4. The full-corpus and scientific evaluation boundaries **remain shut**: full-corpus PII, exact/near-duplicate contamination, provenance/license verification, semantic overlap with the frozen `semantic_behavior_v1` holdout, plus any subsequent training and data acquisition **require separate scoped authorization**. The request to study these checks does not authorize executing them against full corpora. No approval is granted by the present metadata-only evidence.
5. These are **separate original failed-training-lineage preview studies**, not completion evidence for the independent 45-operation tiny-CPU-model staged campaign. The latter remains **11/45** and approval-pending.

## Decision

**HOLD; do not acquire or train.** For structured JSON, target a reproducible filtered or schema-specialized `vara201` subset *without relaxing tokenizer policy* and independently verify source-generation rights; for short answer, test SAF reference-answer question-level grouping/format without collapsing existing split boundaries; for raw continuation, retain WikiText as a prospective preview-level tokenizer-compatible candidate, subject to explicit rights reconciliation and frozen holdout leakage protection. Only after source-specific legal/ethical authorization and an independently scoped full-corpus-access gate should the full-corpus PII, contamination and frozen semantic evaluation leakage checks run. If those pass, request separately scoped `dataset_selection_approval` and `dataset_acquisition` approval decisions.
