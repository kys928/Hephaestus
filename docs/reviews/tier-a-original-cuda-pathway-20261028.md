## Stage B: original model-role observation wiring (#136)

`prepare_native_tier_a_cuda_role_observations` wraps only five native scientific role substeps—Controller entry, Diagnosis, Planner ranking, Evaluator comparison, and Judge verdict. It preserves the original 40 genuine domain-operation delegates and append-only state. For each role, it hashes the **actually produced domain observation**, invokes the original certified model on `cuda:0`, checks its persisted inference receipt, and attaches the returned provenance as an **advisory-only** record. A CPU-mode receipt, missing role revision/adapter hash, rejected deterministic role gate, or unapproved resource scope stops execution.

GPU inference is behind a **separate** `StagedApprovalService` for resource admission. The original `InternalResearchApprovalService` may automatically approve only the exact Tier A *dataset acquisition*; it cannot approve GPU spend. Each CUDA role invocation needs an operation/role/run/source-bound approved receipt from that independent provider. No trained model supplies its own GPU grant.
