# Anvil operator-directed execution policy

**State: paused pending operator direction after balance recovery.** No automatic launch when credit changes. Existing activated commits are immutable; their independent cleanup remains enabled.

## Authorization and scope

An explicit operator instruction to launch a defined mission is the authorization for that mission. Do not introduce a second confirmation, arbitrary GPU preference, datacenter pin, hardcoded hourly cap, or an invented approval step. Preserve explicit operator spending/time/scope limits; do not convert total expenditure into an unrelated hourly restriction. Where no separate hourly ceiling was requested, derive cost feasibility from total budget, actual enforced lease, and authenticated available funding. Do not enable automatic payment or modify unrelated resources to force feasibility.

## Real execution boundaries

Preserve model/runtime compatibility, authenticated funding and provider availability, exact Global Volume identity, resource ownership, independent cleanup, reproducible evidence and protection against duplicate provider creates. A lost provider acknowledgement requires reconciliation, not another create. Read-only catalog rechecks and global reselection are allowed within the approved mission. An exhausted balance is a real blocker, not an approval question.

## Remaining migration

The historical Anvil pilot still has a fixed one-use grant identifier and a frozen investigation-specific implementation. Its immutable consumption records must remain. A reusable operator-launch interface must replace this pilot-specific activation mechanism with per-launch identifiers and explicit scope, allowing a later explicitly authorized mission without rewriting code, resetting prior records, or requesting redundant approval. This migration is not implemented by the cost-policy correction and must be qualified before reuse. No paid execution is triggered by this document.
