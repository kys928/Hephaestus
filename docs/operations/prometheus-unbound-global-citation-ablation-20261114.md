## Interpretation and limits

The format-only alteration was effective in the two observed trials **at the citation interface**, changing rejection for a natural-language source description into an accepted, exactly grounded seven-key inference receipt. This does **not establish statistical reliability**, independence of random noise, or decision causality. The scientific decision remains a failure under both tested conditions, for different reasons. The second result must not be relabeled as an admissible scientific diagnosis.

The scientific constraint is correct: previously exposed six-state parent performance and nonmatching eight-state D failures do not establish any production-parent evaluation-integrity failure. The model's confident repair recommendation is unsupported. Altering independent hypotheses, weakening the admission gate, automatically replacing a model choice, or telling the model to choose `inconclusive` would invalidate the experiment.

**No further paid attempt is justified solely to prompt a desired answer.** Next step: independently review the Diagnostician's reasoning against actual candidate hypotheses and collect authentic matched development generalization evidence; separately test model calibration and counterfactual dependence on *fresh* admissible evidence with a precommitted protocol. Reassess before any additional expense. This project has so far demonstrated real hardware-backed, authentic and provenance-preserved model execution with a working independent veto, **not** autonomous Granite repair.
