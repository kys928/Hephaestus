## External decisions and ownership

An independent scientific reviewer must approve the final prospective protocol
and its exact SHA-256 before any final material is generated or exposed. The
protocol must identify the selected-procedure estimand, three-seed secondary
claims, endpoint, family/semantic/template sampling assumptions, sample size and
power, termination/regression rules, multiplicity, stopping and inconclusive
outcomes. A proposed protocol or a passing pilot is not that approval.

A genuinely separate custodian owns the final generator seed, material sealing,
evaluation machine and Ed25519 grant key. Configure its trusted public key and
fingerprint out of band. Independently operate a durable exposure service outside
the research operator's backup/restore/admin boundary. Pin that service's separate
receipt-signing identity and namespace out of band. The service must survive
campaign restoration, whole operator-environment rollback, process death and
lost acknowledgements. A local witness or colocated fixture ledger does not meet
this institutional requirement. No production service or entrusted final keys
are configured in this repository.

## Irreversible service interface

`state.exposure_authority.ExposureLedger.consume_once(reservation, grant,
submission=...)` is the adapter contract. The institution must provide and
validate its independently operated implementation; the repository supplies only
an explicitly nonconfirmatory fixture implementation. Confirmatory execution is
blocked before sample/model/selection access when this configured external
authority and separately entrusted namespace/keys are absent or fixture-scoped.

The service must independently verify the grant and the complete submission's
binding of material, protocol and model scope. In one durable transaction, enforce
global uniqueness of material commitment, grant and study across renamed studies,
changed nonces and restored operator state. Retain immutable consumed history and
monotonic sequence outside operator rollback. Sign a receipt only for the first
reservation, binding a fresh unpredictable attempt nonce, request/material
commitments, service namespace, authority scope, sequence and UTC issue time.
Retries and receipt-read APIs must never reissue a generation permission. A
read-only historical receipt is evidence, not a fresh permission.

The runner persists local consumed intent **before** the service call, verifies
the fresh signed external receipt and persists it **before** any generation.
Unknown remote acknowledgement, malformed/incorrect authority, failed local
receipt publication, partial generation, hard crash and incomplete reporting all
remain spent/blocked. A local restore cannot erase remote consumption, and a
captured signed receipt cannot authorize a new nonce. Never reset either ledger
to recover an exposed batch. Independent adjudication may mark it unusable; any
replacement study requires newly approved materials and protocol, not a retry.
