# Stage policy — stable reference path

The eight-phase control spine enforces ordered role boundaries while stage-specific profiles define entry conditions, data/eval policy, failure triggers, escalation and exit decisions. An experiment's 45 native operations are not the same as its eight control phases.

Refer to [the architecture and phase guide](architecture/control-and-governance.md), [autonomous experiment contracts](autonomous_experiment_loop.md), [lineage policy](lineage_policy.md) and [safe operating procedure](operations/run-safety.md). No role may change stage, training materialization or frozen evaluation identity without typed evidence and valid policy.
